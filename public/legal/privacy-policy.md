# Privacy Policy

**Last Updated: November 7, 2026**

MyPrayerTower ("we," "our," or "us") is an independent Catholic digital devotional platform based in Mumbai, India, serving the global faithful since 2018. This Privacy Policy details how we collect, handle, and safeguard your personal information across our website (myprayertower.com) and mobile applications.

---

## 1. Information We Collect

### 1.1 Information You Provide
- **Account Data:** Name, email address, encrypted password hash (via Supabase Auth), profile avatar.
- **Prayer Intentions:** Petitions and intentions posted to the Global Prayer Wall (can be public or anonymous).
- **Devotional Requests:** Names and intention notes submitted for Mass offerings, virtual candles, spiritual bouquets, or memorial plaques.
- **Support Messages:** Direct inquiries submitted via contact forms or email.

### 1.2 Automatically Collected Data
- **Device & Geospatial:** Device model, operating system, IP address (used solely for locating nearby Catholic churches and Mass times).
- **Usage Metrics:** Liturgical pages visited, prayer streaks, audio meditation session durations.
- **Cookies & Local Cache:** Session tokens, theme settings, and offline scripture cache.

### 1.3 Biometrics & Audio (Mobile App)
- **Biometric Authentication:** Fingerprint and Face ID unlock occur strictly on-device via native Android/iOS security hardware. Biometric data is never transmitted to or stored on our servers.
- **Audio & Media:** Catholic Sleep Stories and sacred audio are streamed or cached locally. No microphone access or audio recording is used.

---

## 2. Payment Data & Processing
- **No Stored Card Data:** We never store or handle raw credit card numbers or banking credentials.
- **Processors:** Transactions are handled via **Google Play Billing (Library 8.0+)** for Android and **PayPal** for web.
- **Commercial Service Export:** Remittances are legally categorized as commercial service fees for digital platform access and devotional coordination (SAC Code).

---

## 3. Devotional Offering Distribution (70/20/10)
Devotional contributions are apportioned transparently:
- **70% Church & Clergy:** Direct Mass stipends forwarded to partner Catholic priests and altar maintenance.
- **20% Pastoral Care:** Intention coordination, digital certificate verification, and user pastoral support.
- **10% Infrastructure:** Secure cloud hosting, SSL encryption, database integrity, and mobile app maintenance.

*Note: All deliverables are 100% digital. Remittances are non-tax-deductible.*

---

## 4. Data Protection & Global Privacy Rights (GDPR & CCPA)
- **No Sale of Data:** We never sell, rent, or monetize your personal or spiritual data to third-party data brokers.
- **Your Rights:** You have the right to request access, correction, data portability, or permanent deletion of your account and records at any time.
- **Data Deletion:** Contact privacy@myprayertower.com to request complete data erasure (processed within 30 days).
