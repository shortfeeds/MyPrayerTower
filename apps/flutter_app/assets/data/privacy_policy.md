## 1. Introduction
MyPrayerTower ("we," "our," or "us") is an independent Catholic digital media and devotional technology provider based in Mumbai, India, serving the global faithful since 2018. This Privacy Policy explains how we collect, handle, and safeguard your personal information when using our mobile applications and digital platforms.

## 2. Information We Collect

### 2.1 Information You Provide
- **Account Data:** Name, email address, and encrypted password hash (managed via Supabase Auth).
- **Prayer Content:** Intentions and petitions submitted to the Global Prayer Wall (can be public or anonymous).
- **Devotional Intentions:** Names and prayer intentions submitted for Mass offerings, virtual candles, or personalized spiritual bouquet certificates.
- **Support Inquiries:** Direct messages sent to customer support.

### 2.2 Automatically Collected Data
- **Device Attributes:** Operating system version, hardware model, anonymous crash metrics.
- **Approximate Location:** Used exclusively with your permission to locate nearby Catholic churches and Mass times. Location data is never stored on or tracked by our remote servers.
- **Notification Tokens:** Device tokens for delivering optional daily scripture reflections and feast day alerts.

### 2.3 Biometrics & Audio Playback
- **Biometric Security:** Biometric authentication (fingerprint/Face ID) operates strictly on-device using Android/iOS hardware security modules. We never transmit, store, or have access to your biometric identifiers.
- **Catholic Sleep Stories & Audio:** Narratives and ambient chimes stream or cache locally for offline meditation. No microphone access or audio recording is used.

## 3. Devotional Offering Distribution (70/20/10)
Devotional contributions are apportioned transparently:
- **70% Church & Clergy:** Direct Mass stipends forwarded to partner Catholic priests and altar sanctuary upkeep.
- **20% Pastoral Care:** Intention coordination, digital certificate verification, and user pastoral support.
- **10% Infrastructure:** SSL encryption, cloud database integrity, and mobile app maintenance.

*Legal Classification:* MyPrayerTower facilitates devotional services as commercial digital platform fees (SAC Code). Payments are not tax-deductible charitable contributions. All services are 100% digital with no physical shipment.

## 4. Payment Data & Google Play Billing 8.0
- **No Stored Financial Data:** We never collect or store credit card numbers or bank credentials.
- **Google Play Billing (Library 8.0+):** All Android purchases and subscriptions are tokenized and processed securely through Google Play Billing in full compliance with Google Play monetization policies.

## 5. Data Protection & Privacy Rights (GDPR & CCPA)
- **No Sale of Data:** We never sell, rent, or trade your personal or prayer data.
- **Your Rights:** You have the right to request access, correction, export, or permanent deletion of your account and submitted prayer records at any time.
- **Data Erasure:** Email privacy@myprayertower.com to request permanent deletion within 30 days.
