# Privacy Policy

**Last Updated: October 22, 2026**

MyPrayerTower ("we," "our," or "us") is dedicated to honoring and protecting your privacy as you engage in devotion and prayer. This Privacy Policy details how we collect, handle, and safeguard your information across our website and mobile apps.

---

## 1. Information We Collect

### 1.1 Information You Provide
- **Account Data:** Name, email address, password hash, and spiritual preferences.
- **Prayer Content:** Intentions posted to the Global Prayer Wall (can be public or anonymous).
- **Devotional Intentions:** Names and prayer intentions submitted for Mass offerings or virtual candles.
- **Support Messages:** Direct inquiries sent through our contact forms.

### 1.2 Automatically Collected Information
- **Device & Connection:** Browser type, operating system, IP address (used for locating nearby Catholic churches).
- **Usage Metrics:** Visited pages, devotional tools accessed, session duration.
- **Cookies & Cache:** Local storage and session cookies for remembering prayers and theme preferences.

---

## 2. Payment Data & Processing

- **No Stored Card Data:** We never collect or store full credit card numbers or bank credentials on our servers.
- **Processors:** Payments are processed securely through **PayPal** (for web) and **Google Play Billing** (for Android).
- **Service Classification:** Payments are recorded as commercial service fees for digital platform access and devotional coordination.

---

## 3. Data Protection & GDPR / CCPA Compliance

- **No Sale of Personal Data:** We do not sell, rent, or trade your personal or prayer data to third-party data brokers.
- **Your Rights:** You have the right to request access, correction, or permanent deletion of your account and submitted prayer records at any time.
- **Contact:** To exercise your privacy rights, email privacy@myprayertower.com.
